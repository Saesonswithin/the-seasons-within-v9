THE SEASONS WITHIN — EASY OPEN / ANY INSTALLED PYTHON

1. Extract this ZIP completely.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click: DOUBLE CLICK TO OPEN THE SEASONS WITHIN.bat

The launcher uses a real Python already installed on the computer (including newer versions such as Python 3.15) and does NOT require Python 3.12 specifically.

It installs the core packages needed to open the platform and then opens:
http://127.0.0.1:5055

ASTROLOGY NOTE:
The platform will still open if the local Swiss Ephemeris package does not yet provide a prebuilt wheel for your newest Python version. In that case, the birth-chart calculation screen will remain unavailable until a compatible Swiss Ephemeris package/runtime is connected. Signup, profiles, Stripe-ready billing, Business Network, Business App Builder, Retreats, messages, and the rest of the platform continue to work.
