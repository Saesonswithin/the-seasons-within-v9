THE SEASONS WITHIN — SIMPLIFIED FUNCTIONAL UPDATE
Packaged: 2026-08-22

This is the complete existing application with its approved visual design preserved.

Included functional corrections:

- Permanent natal wheel calculated from birth date, time, place, coordinates,
  and historical timezone.
- Exact zodiac-longitude plotting for Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter,
  Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto.
- Ascendant, houses, and house cusps only when birth time and coordinates are reliable.
- Stable deterministic Conscious Coordination percentages based on psychological
  dimensions and natal supporting information, not daily or monthly transits.
- One professional personal profile interpretation that regenerates when relevant
  profile or birth evidence changes.
- Two separate side-by-side natal wheels for member compatibility.
- Love / Relationship, Friendship, and Business compatibility only.
- Compatibility excludes private Journal information and uses evidence fingerprints.
- One active Lunar Season Reflection tied to the Moon's actual zodiac-sign ingress.
- One written personalized meditation tied to that Lunar Season Reflection.
- Previous Lunar Season Reflections are saved in private Journal history.
- Separate daily reports, monthly planetary reports, Reiki Reflection, report
  narration, MP3 report audio, and browser Read Aloud are retired.
- Community, Journal, Journal Inbox, messaging, Notifications, Preferred Dates,
  Business, Retreat, Membership, affiliate, administrator, and database systems
  remain preserved.
- Existing Business Journal extended in place with Licenses & Certifications,
  Funding Opportunities, Grant & Loan Search, Saved Opportunities, and Proposal Builder.
- Funding search reads current public Grants.gov listings and links to official notices.
  Official SBA Lender Match and Microloan pathways are included without inventing awards.
- Funding match explanations and proposal drafts reuse the member's saved Business Plan
  intake, industry, stage, needs, audience, mission, and goals.
- Business Plan generation is split into three checkpointed AI stages so Render worker
  timeouts do not erase the questionnaire or completed sections.
- If the external AI service is unavailable or rejects its credential, the app still
  creates and saves a complete strategic Business Plan from the questionnaire. The
  plan derives an offer architecture, launch sequence, customer strategy, revenue
  ladder, funding allocation, implementation workstreams, risks, KPIs, decision gates,
  and documentation roadmap instead of merely rewriting questionnaire answers.
- Marketing Strategy and 90-Day Launch Plan are substantial standalone documents with
  their own channel rules, measurement systems, weekly execution, and decision gates.
- Failed Business Plan versions show Retry instead of empty View/Download controls.
- Marketing Strategy and 90-Day Launch Plan are displayed only from a verified,
  successfully generated and persisted Business Plan version.
- New Business Journal records persist through DATABASE_URL in the connected PostgreSQL
  database. OPENAI_API_KEY remains an environment secret and is never packaged.
