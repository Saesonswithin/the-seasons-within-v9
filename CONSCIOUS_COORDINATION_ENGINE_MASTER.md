# The Seasons Within — Simplified Intelligence Contract

This is a functional contract beneath the approved application. Do not redesign, rearrange, recolor or replace the existing pages, navigation, cards, Community, Business, Retreat, messaging, Notifications, Preferred Dates or Journal architecture.

## Three separate systems

### 1. Permanent natal astrology

The natal wheel displays the factual sky at the member's birth. It uses birth date, birth time, birth city, region, country, coordinates and the correct historical IANA timezone.

- Calculate and plot Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune and Pluto from exact zodiac longitude.
- Include Ascendant, houses and house cusps only when birth time and coordinates are reliable.
- Unknown birth time never invents Rising or houses. A time-sensitive Moon is identified rather than treated as certain.
- The zodiac ring uses a conventional fixed orientation. Only factual birth information determines planetary placement.
- Current Moon movement, transits, Journal entries and psychological answers never move natal planets.
- Recalculate only when saved birth source information changes or an inaccurate source is corrected.

### 2. Stable Conscious Coordination Profile

The personal profile answers: **How does this member tend to coordinate psychologically, emotionally and relationally?**

- Deterministic Python calculates the category percentages and Overall Conscious Coordination percentage.
- Evidence includes relevant psychological answers and approved natal supporting patterns.
- AI never chooses or changes a percentage.
- AI receives structured evidence and writes a professional one- or two-paragraph interpretation of emotional processing, communication, connection, boundaries, strengths and areas requiring awareness.
- Do not merely repeat answers or write generic zodiac paragraphs.
- No current Moon, transits, daily evidence, monthly evidence, meditation or private Journal material enters this stable profile.
- A profile version changes only when relevant profile/connection answers or birth information changes.

### 3. Lunar Journal

The Lunar Season Reflection answers: **What may deserve attention during the Moon's current zodiac period based on the member's natal pattern and current lived themes?**

- Calculate the current Moon's exact longitude from the astrology engine.
- Determine its zodiac sign from longitude.
- Find and store the actual refined zodiac-boundary ingress time; never use a fixed 2.5-day timer.
- Keep one active reflection until the Moon actually enters the next sign.
- On a real sign transition, calculate the new Moon position, meaningful correspondence to natal Moon, Sun, reliable Rising, Mercury, Venus and Mars, structured recent private Journal themes and relevant regulation preferences.
- AI receives structured Journal themes only. Raw entries are never quoted, repeated or identified as the source.
- Classify one symbolic Season Within from the combined evidence: Spring — Renewal; Summer — Expansion & Connection; Autumn — Reflection & Release; or Winter — Restoration & Inner Listening. This is not the outdoor calendar season.
- Save the new reflection to private Journal history and make it active.
- Generate one written personalized meditation from that exact reflection.

There is no separate daily report, monthly planetary report collection, Reiki Reflection, report narration, automatic MP3 report audio or browser text-to-speech in this simplified system.

## Member-to-member compatibility

Compatibility exists only when a logged-in participating member views another participating member.

- Show two separate natal wheels side-by-side: **Your Wheel** and **Member's Wheel**.
- Never merge, overlay or move either member's planets.
- Offer **View Our Conscious Coordination / View Compatibility** beneath the wheel area using the existing design.
- Use only three connection types: Love / Relationship, Friendship and Business.
- Default emphasis comes from what the viewing member says they are seeking, while allowing those three views to be selected.
- Python calculates the shared percentage from factual chart-to-chart comparison and both permitted psychological profiles.
- AI interprets why the two profiles may coordinate, where timing or expectations differ, and what practical behavior may help.
- Reports stay understandable; backend aspects may be exact, but member-facing language does not expose degrees, orbs or technical synastry mechanics.
- Compatibility caches are fingerprinted to both current natal charts, both permitted profiles, category and connection type so changed evidence regenerates the report.

## Compatibility focus

- **Love / Relationship:** emotional connection, communication, affection, attraction, trust, boundaries, conflict, repair and relationship values.
- **Friendship:** communication, emotional rhythm, trust, social energy, interests, mutual support and boundaries.
- **Business:** communication, leadership, working style, responsibility, reliability, decisions, conflict, collaboration and values.

Do not create Family, Retreat or generic compatibility types in the updated member experience.

## Privacy

- The natal wheel and personal Coordination Profile belong to the member.
- Private Journal entries belong only to the Journal owner.
- Compatibility receives no private Journal text, themes, summaries or inferred confidential answers.
- Another member sees only information permitted by existing profile/privacy settings.
- Interpret confidential coordination evidence without exposing answers verbatim.
